# Helping test Sherlog

Thank you for helping. Sherlog is a free app that explains, in plain words, why a PC crashed. To check that its
explanations are right, it needs crash records from real PCs like yours. This takes about 5 minutes.

Sherlog only reads. **It doesn't change anything on your PC**, and it doesn't send anything by itself: you decide
whether to send the file it makes.

## 1. Unzip it

Right-click the zip file you were sent and choose **Extract All**, then **Extract**. A folder opens with
`sherlog.exe` and a `library` folder in it. Keep them together.

## 2. Double-click sherlog.exe

This test version isn't signed yet, so Windows doesn't recognize it and shows a blue box:
**"Windows protected your PC"**.

1. Click **More info**.
2. Click **Run anyway**.

If Windows or your antivirus blocks it and there's no **Run anyway** button, stop there and tell the person who sent
you Sherlog. Please don't turn off your antivirus or any other protection for this.

## 3. Answer the questions

A black window opens and asks 7 short questions about your crashes. For each one, type the number of your answer and
press **Enter**. For some questions you can type more than one number, like `1 3`. To skip a question, just press
**Enter**. The last question lets you type anything else that might help.

When it's done, the window tells you where it saved the file. Press **Enter** to close it.

## 4. Send the file

On your **Desktop** there's now a file named like `sherlog-evidence-2026-12-01.zip`. Send it to the person who sent
you Sherlog, by email or chat. It's small.

If the window said something went wrong, send a photo of the window instead.

## What's in the file

Before saving, Sherlog replaces your user name, your PC's name, your account ID and any email address with
placeholders. The file still shows which apps or games crashed and when, which apps, drivers and Windows updates were
installed in the last 90 days, your Windows version, and your hardware (processor, graphics card, memory). It also has
your answers to the questions.

You can check it before you send it: double-click the zip to look inside, and open any file in it with Notepad.

The person who sent you Sherlog may ask you a week later whether the crashes stopped. That's the most useful answer of
all.
